Good evening, everyone. Tonight's question is about the Ponta Serena lighthouse.

The lighthouse was built in eighteen fifty-two by the engineer [[?? tarbaldo vrzeni ]], who came from the Island of Gulls. The stones were laid without mortar and still withstood three great storms. The keeper lived with his family in a two-room house beside the tower and climbed one hundred and twenty steps every night to light the [[? óleo de baleia ⟶ suggestion: whale-oil lamp (confirm on audio) ]]. The light reached twelve miles, and the fishermen said it blinked like a tired eye.

When the lighthouse was decommissioned in nineteen seventy, the lens was taken to the town museum, where it remains today, and the tower stood empty for twenty years, until a residents' association decided to look after it.
